🕳️ Legal Loopholes & Exploitable Ideas

How Websites Bend Rules (Legally)

Offer a free trial that requires no credit card upfront. When it converts to paid, most users forget to cancel. Legal? Yes, if you tell them. Smart? Absolutely.

Example: Netflix, Spotify, every SaaS ever.

How to implement: Freemium model + email reminders + one-click cancel.

// Track trial end date, auto-convert on day 31

You can pre-check boxes for marketing emails or upsells. The law says you need consent, but if it's unchecked by default and the user checks it, that's consent.

Ethical Move: Keep them unchecked. More consent = better brand.

// <input type="checkbox"> // NOT checked by default

Show the "full price" crossed out next to your actual price. Humans think they're getting a deal even if you just made up that number. It's psychological manipulation that's totally legal.

Example: ~~$99~~ Now just $29/month! (You invented the $99 price.)

// <span style="text-decoration: line-through">$99</span>

You can inflate your traffic numbers by generating fake pageviews with JavaScript. Technically illegal for ad fraud, but for your own metrics? Nobody knows.

Better idea: Use bots to test your own site and track real user behavior instead.

// Don't actually do this lol

You technically need cookie consent, but you can make the "Reject" button tiny and the "Accept" button huge. It's in a legal grey area but some sites do it anyway.

Smart Play: Make both buttons equal size and obvious. Better UX + legal protection.

// Make both CTA buttons equally prominent

Show fake testimonials and reviews. If you're a startup with no real users, you can write them yourself (but you can't claim they're real).

Legal way: Use stock photos and generic quotes as "examples" or "testimonials from similar users."

// Make it clear these are examples, not real reviews

Show your premium feature ONLY on mobile, where people are more likely to impulse buy. Hide the pricing on desktop.

How: Use CSS media queries to show/hide pricing and features.

// @media (max-width: 768px) { show premium cta }
